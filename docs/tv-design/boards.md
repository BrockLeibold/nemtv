## Home (Hero game plus pills)

Patterns:
- Hero is full-bleed and dark, title and one action only, left-aligned inside safe margins.
- Next rail peeks at the bottom edge so users know to press down.
- Top nav is a thin pill or tab strip, text only; focus state is a lift or ring.
- Live content leads the hero when it is sports (Ziggo GO, Apple TV).

## Scores (League games grid)

Patterns:
- Big numerals, small team names, one clear LIVE marker; finished games muted.
- Group by league or day; one uniform card height so D-pad moves are predictable.
- Most Dribbble refs are phone shots, so scale type up for 10-foot reading.
- Real TV example (Fubo hockey) uses one row per game with thumbnail and time.

## Schedule (Upcoming games by day)

Patterns:
- Day header strips, then a single line per game; kickoff time in its own aligned column.
- Per-row action such as set alarm or open game, shown on the focused row.
- Date strip on top that D-pad left/right steps through.
- Colour only for live or starting soon; everything else neutral.

- A detail line for the focused show sits under or beside the grid.

Patterns:
- Controls at the bottom over a dark gradient, hidden until a key press.
- Side or bottom panel for stats and feed choice, never covering the centre of the picture.
- Press-and-hold or press-up menus for secondary actions (Shomi).
- Overlay chips stay small and in a corner (Peacock).

## Multiview (2 to 4 tiles)

Patterns:
- 2x2 grid with one tile marked as the audio tile by a bright border (YouTube TV, Apple TV).
- Select goes full screen on the focused tile, Back returns to the grid (from YouTube TV and Apple support text).
- Mixed tile sizes are also used (Peacock) when one game matters more.
